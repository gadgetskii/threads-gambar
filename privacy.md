# Privacy Policy — Automation Shopee Affiliate

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

This privacy policy covers the app **Automation Shopee Affiliate**, a personal tool run by the owner of the Facebook Page "No.1 BEST IPHONE & Accessories" and the Threads/Instagram account satuuno1 (gadgetskii).

## What the app does
The app only posts content (text, images and videos about Shopee affiliate products) to the owner's own Facebook Page and Threads account, replies to comments on the owner's own posts, and reads basic performance statistics (views, likes, replies) of those posts.

## Data we access
- Content of posts and comments on the owner's own Page and Threads account, used only to reply to comments and to make performance reports for the owner.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- We do not collect personal data from other users beyond the public comment text needed to write a reply.

## Data storage
Post records and statistics are stored in a private repository controlled by the owner. Comment text may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic Claude) only to draft a short reply, and is not stored by the app afterwards.

## Data deletion
To ask for any data related to you to be deleted, email **gadgetskii@gmail.com** with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete it within 30 days.
